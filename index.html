The Page 6 Keyword Stuffing Exploit: pizza-pizza-pizza.com repeats the keyword "pizza" exactly 10 times in raw text. Under early 1998 search algorithms, this page easily ranked #1 because frequency was the dominant metric. However, Page 6 has 0 incoming links from any other page. Under the Random Surfer PageRank algorithm, Page 6 ranks dead last (#6) with only ~2.4% share, visited almost exclusively when the surfer makes a random 15% teleportation jump. PageRank eliminated keyword stuffing by measuring peer link authority.
